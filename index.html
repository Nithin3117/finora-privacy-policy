Finora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Welcome to Finora. This Privacy Policy explains how Finora ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Finora personal finance application.

1. Information We Collect

Finora may collect and process the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information such as your email address when you create or use an account.
  • Financial information that you enter into the app, such as income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, categories, and transaction details.
  • App settings such as your selected currency, reminders, and preferences.
  • Information required to provide notifications and reminders that you enable.
  • Technical information necessary for the app to operate correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through Finora to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's features.
  • Store and synchronize your financial data.
  • Display your transactions, budgets, savings goals, and financial summaries.
  • Provide currency conversion and related financial calculations.
  • Send reminders and notifications that you have enabled.
  • Improve the performance, reliability, and security of the app.
  • Provide account authentication and account-related functionality.

3. Financial Information

Finora allows you to enter personal financial information, including income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, and transaction details. This information is used to provide the financial tracking features of the app.

Finora does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. The calculations and information displayed by the app are intended for personal tracking and informational purposes only.

4. Data Storage

Depending on the feature and your account status, information may be stored locally on your device and/or securely stored using our backend services.

Finora uses Supabase services for certain backend functions, including authentication, data storage, and synchronization.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Finora against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Notifications

Finora may use notification services to provide reminders and other notifications that you choose to enable within the app.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate Finora, such as backend, authentication, database, hosting, and notification service providers.

Such services may process information only as necessary to provide their services and operate the app.

8. Third-Party Services

Finora may use third-party services to provide functionality such as authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, notifications, analytics, or other technical services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

10. Account and Data Deletion

If Finora provides account deletion functionality, you may use the available account deletion option within the app.

You may also contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal information. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

Finora is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Finora, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Finora Google Play listing.
