Finora Account Deletion

This page explains how Finora users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Finora account, open the Finora app and go to Settings → Account Settings. Use the available account deletion option to submit your request.

Important: Account deletion may permanently remove your account and associated financial data. This action may not be reversible.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information
  • Financial transactions
  • Budgets
  • Savings goals
  • Reminders and related account data

Data Retention

Data associated with the deleted account will be removed according to Finora's data retention and deletion procedures.

Contact

If you need help with account deletion, please contact the Finora support team through the contact information provided in the app.
